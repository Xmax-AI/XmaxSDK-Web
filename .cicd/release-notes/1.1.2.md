## What's New

- Enjoy consistent beauty effects across live video and recordings, with adjustable smoothing, brightening, sharpening, and redness.
- More reliable generation starts and live scene updates, even on unstable connections.
- Check your remaining credits directly in your app, making it easier to keep track of usage.

## API Changes

- Add `XmaxClient.getCreditBalance()` for account balance queries.
- Add `setResolvedMediaSizeListener()` for resolved media dimensions.
- Replace view-level beauty APIs with `setRemoteVideoBeauty()` and `setRemoteVideoBeautyErrorListener()` on the realtime manager. Rename `VideoBeautyOptions` to `RealtimeVideoBeautyOptions`; omitted strengths default to `0`.
- Custom models now follow `XmaxConfiguration.environment`.
